    Layers: A bottom 1st, B bottom 2nd, C top 3rd, D top 4th. Every mat must say which way it runs: E-W or N-S, one direction per layer; nothing is assumed when the drawing is silent. Also understood: N12 @ 200 CTS A E-W, N12-150 C NS.

    Openings · mm from grid 1 / grid A
    Trimmers each side T&B follow the opening size.
    Set-downs · mm from grid 1 / grid A, depth
    Shallow (≤ 160): the mats stop at each edge and Z-bars splice across, T&B.
    Step · slab raised NE of a corner, on a nib
    Over 160: L-bars lap each mat on both sides of the step; the nib is the step's vertical face.
    Balconies · edge, from / to along it, out, thk, set-down
    From / to run along the edge: mm from grid 1 on the N and S edges, from grid A on the E and W edges.

      How to read it

      Each tag is one run of bars, written like the placing drawing: count, size, spacing, layer. The tick-ended line under it shows which bars it covers.

      Plan gives a true top-down floor plan with grids and dimensions. Step through the layers in fixing order: the layer being placed is solid, the ones below are ghosted.

      2D placing sheet draws the current layer as a placing drawing: bars as lines, cogs as ticks, grids with dimension strings, and the first and last bar line dimensioned from the nearest grid. Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, double-click to fit, click a bar for its details. A schedule row highlights that mark on the sheet with its labels. Print sheet puts the layer on A3 landscape at a standard scale.

      Site mode (top right) is the tablet view for fixing: one layer at a time, big tags, tap a bar for its mark, cut length, setout from the grids and laps.
