      How to read it

      Each tag is one run of bars, written like the placing drawing: count, size, spacing, layer. The tick-ended line under it shows which bars it covers.

      Plan gives a true top-down floor plan with grids and dimensions. Step through the layers in fixing order: the layer being placed is solid, the ones below are ghosted.

      Site mode (top right) is the tablet view for fixing: one layer at a time, big tags, tap a bar for its mark, cut length, setout from the grids and laps.
