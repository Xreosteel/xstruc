Bars: one line each way, H or V, outer layer first (N12-200 H, then N12-200 V). Mesh: one line, its long wires H or V (SL82 H, RL918 V). Nothing is assumed when a line is silent.

    Openings · mm from the bottom-left corner
    x from the left edge, y from the bottom edge, looking down on the panel on its bed. An opening that reaches an edge is a door or notch: write 0 (or the panel size) for that side; the perimeter bar stops at it.
    Inserts · lifters, brace inserts, ferrules
    Positions from the erection designer's drawing: an edge anchor by x (top / bottom edge) or y (left / right edge), a face insert by x, y on NF or FF. Checked against the supplier catalogue; failed checks are flagged, never fixed.

    Panel sheet · approval block
    Printed in the approval block on the panel sheet (2D placing sheet, Inserts step or Finished panel); the date lines stay blank to be signed by hand.

    Layers: A bottom 1st, B bottom 2nd, C top 3rd, D top 4th. Every mat must say which way it runs: E-W or N-S, one direction per layer; nothing is assumed when the drawing is silent. Also understood: N12 @ 200 CTS A E-W, N12-150 C NS. Mesh: SL82 T E-W lays 6.0 × 2.4 sheets over both top layers (C+D) with the long wires E-W, lapped and cut clear of openings; RL918 B N-S the bottom.

    Openings · mm from grid 1 / grid A
    Trimmers each side T&B follow the opening size.
    Set-downs · mm from grid 1 / grid A, depth
    Shallow (≤ 160): the mats stop at each edge and Z-bars splice across, T&B.
    Step · slab raised NE of a corner, on a nib
    Over 160: L-bars lap each mat on both sides of the step; the nib is the step's vertical face.
    Stair · straight flight up from the floor below into a void
    Risers 115–190, goings 250–355, 2G + R 550–700, up to 18 risers (NCC D3D14). The flight descends from the top riser back across the void and on under the slab; the top bars are cast into this slab, the flight is a later pour. Waist and bar sizes are placeholders until the engineer's detail is entered.
    Balconies · edge, from / to along it, out, thk, set-down
    From / to run along the edge: mm from grid 1 on the N and S edges, from grid A on the E and W edges.

      How to read it

      Each tag is one run of bars, written like the placing drawing: count, size, spacing, layer. The tick-ended line under it shows which bars it covers.

      Plan gives a true top-down floor plan with grids and dimensions. Step through the layers in fixing order: the layer being placed is solid, the ones below are ghosted.

      2D placing sheet draws the current layer as a placing drawing: bars as lines, cogs as ticks, grids with dimension strings, and the first and last bar line dimensioned from the nearest grid. Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, double-click to fit, click a bar for its details. A schedule row highlights that mark on the sheet with its labels. Print sheet puts the layer on A3 landscape at a standard scale.

      A precast panel has two poses: On the bed as it is cast and fixed, or Standing on its bottom edge with the face that was up on the bed towards you; Plan becomes a true elevation when it stands.

      Section cut slices the model on a vertical E–W or N–S plane: the 3D view is clipped at it and the true section is drawn under the stage with every layer's level and the cover to the nearest face. Drag the slider to move the cut, look the other way, zoom the strip, click a cut bar for its details.

      Site mode (top right) is the tablet view for fixing: one layer at a time, big tags, tap a bar for its mark, cut length, setout from the grids and laps.
